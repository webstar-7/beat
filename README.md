# Beat · NYANSATEK

A beat maker and frequency lab for learner sound engineers. Runs entirely in the browser (Web Audio API), works offline, and installs as an app.

Live: https://webstar-7.github.io/beat/ · Also in the Resources hub: https://webstar-7.github.io/resources/#beat

## Beat Maker
- 10-track step sequencer (kick, snare, clap, closed/open hat with choke, rim, shaker, conga, tom, tuned 808), 8–32 steps
- Synthesized kit you can shape (tune, decay, tone), or load your own samples per track
- 808 notes per step with glide, key/scale helper, A = 440 or 432 Hz tuning
- Per-step velocity, swing, tap tempo, mute/solo, volume and pan
- Four patterns (A–D) and a song chain
- Style starters: Afrobeats, Amapiano, Highlife/Hiplife, Trap, Boom bap, House
- Master glue compressor, limiter, level meter and spectrum
- Export: mixed WAV, dry stems (one WAV per track), and MIDI (GM drums on ch 10, 808 on ch 1); 16/24-bit, 44.1/48 kHz, loop-ready option
- Save projects in the browser or as a file (samples included)

## Frequency Lab
- Tone generator for any frequency 1–20,000 Hz with facts: period, wavelength, nearest note and cents, mix band, ear sensitivity, relative energy
- Brainwave beats: binaural, isochronic and monaural (delta to gamma, Schumann 7.83 Hz)
- Drone pad in just intonation, noise beds (white, pink, brown), reverb
- Intent presets: sleep, meditation (Om 136.1 Hz), relaxation, healing (528 Hz), manifestation, grounding, focus, 40 Hz
- Frequency library with what is measured vs tradition, saved custom sounds, session timer, WAV export

## Learn
Lessons on frequency, energy and loudness, mix bands you can hear, ear training (pure tones and EQ boosts), building beats by genre, brainwave beats, healing frequencies, a note-frequency table, and hearing safety.
